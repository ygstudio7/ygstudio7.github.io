   ### Part A: Fill-in-the-Blank (빈칸 채우기)

   1. The mother tried to __________ her crying child by singing a lullaby.
   2. His __________ attitude during the interview made a poor impression on the manager.
   3. The knight pledged to __________ his lord and protect the kingdom.
   4. The twins were so __________ that even their closest friends had trouble telling them apart.
   5. The lawyer’s __________ argument convinced the jury.

   ### Part B: Synonyms (동의어 고르기)

   2. Which word is the closest in meaning to *congested*?

       b) empty
       c) wide
       d) open
   3. Which word is the closest in meaning to *durable*?
       a) fragile
       b) long-lasting
       c) temporary
       d) weak

   ### Part D: Multiple Choice in Context (문맥에 맞는 단어 고르기)

   1. After losing the match, the coach refused to __________ his players but encouraged them instead.
       a) banter
       b) maim
       c) invalidate
       d) venerate
   2. The students tried to __________ the teacher into giving them less homework.
       a) veer
       b) venerate
       c) invalidate
       d) gingerly
   3. The referee decided to __________ the team’s last goal after reviewing the replay.
       a) invalidate
       b) minimize
       c) maim
       d) venerate

   ### Part F: Sentence Rewrite (문장 바꿔쓰기)
