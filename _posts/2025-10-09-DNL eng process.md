## 1. Message to be an engineer/scientist/researcher

------

**Hi,**

I know you really want to be a strong builder and be recognized as one.
But there’s something important you need to remember: **being a builder is not about claiming the title, it’s about proving it through your work.**

To be recognized as a builder, it’s not enough to just say “I am a builder.”
You need to **show** it by going through the process:

- Identify the problem
- Brainstorm possible solutions
- Research what others have tried
- Choose an idea, build and test it, and see if it works
- If it doesn’t work, research new methods and try again

And here’s the key: don’t just do this in your head. You need to **document the process**.
Why? Because when you write it down, people can actually see how you solved problems and improved. That’s how others will respect you as a builder.

Remember: the builder role is not earned by words, but by **visible results and a clear process.**
I believe you can do this, and this season I want to see you grow by showing your process step by step.

------
