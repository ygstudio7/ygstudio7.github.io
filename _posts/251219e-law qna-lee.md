> # **Legal case**
>
> **Client:** Young Geun Kim
> **Opposing Party:** Wan Koo Lee (Licensed contractor)
>
>  **Properties Involved:**
>
> - 10106 Capistrano Ave, South Gate, CA 90280
> - 8852 Syracuse Ave, Anaheim, CA 92804
>
> ## **Case Summary**
>
> A contractor received a **$10,000 real estate deposit** and a **$15,000 short-term loan**, misused the funds, issued **NSF checks**, repaid only **$5,000**, and disappeared, leaving **$20,000 unpaid** and causing additional financial damages.
>
> ------
>
> ## **Timeline and key facts**
>
> - **2025/06/05** – Mr. Lee presented a South Gate property as a good investment.
> - **2025/06/10** – I paid **$10,000 deposit**; Mr. Lee provided a personal check of the same amount as collateral.
> - **2025/06/11** – Deposit was placed into Mr. Lee's **personal account**, not delivered to seller.
> - **2025/08/09** – Mr. Lee requested a **two-week loan of $15,000** for renovation work on the Anaheim property, representing that the loan would be fully repaid by **late August 2025**, when the property owner paid him for the renovation work. I informed Mr. Lee that these funds were intended to be used for an **escrow closing scheduled for September**. Despite this, I issued **three checks in the amount of $5,000 each**, totaling **$15,000**, and Mr. Lee provided **three personal checks** as purported collateral.
> - **2025/08/14–08/22** – Mr. Lee cashed all loan checks.
> - **2025/08/31** – Repayment deadline missed.
> - **2025/09/04** – Mr. Lee admitted all collateral checks would bounce.
> - **2025/9/19** – Due to continued non-response and urgent escrow needs, I deposited one $5,000 collateral check
> - **2025/09/23** – First collateral check returned **NSF**.
> - **2025/09/24** – Only **$5,000** recovered; **$20,000 remains unpaid**.
> - **2025/09/10–Present** – Mr. Lee stopped all communication.
> - **2025/09/20** – I forced to borrow **$30,000 at 7.25% interest** to save an escrow.
>
