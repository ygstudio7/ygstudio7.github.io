## **3. Statement of Claim**

This action arises from the defendant’s failure to return money entrusted to him, misuse of funds, issuance of non-sufficient-funds (NSF) checks, and abandonment of communication.

Specifically, the defendant:

1. Received **$10,000** from the plaintiff as a real estate deposit based on representations that the funds were required by a seller and would be safely held.
2. Deposited those funds into **his personal bank account** instead of delivering them to the seller.
3. Requested and received an additional **$15,000 short-term loan**, promising repayment within two weeks.
4. Failed to repay the loan as agreed and returned only **$5,000**, leaving **$20,000 unpaid**.
5. Provided **checks that were returned for insufficient funds**, despite assurances that they could be deposited at any time.
6. Ceased all communication and ignored repeated repayment requests.
7. Caused significant financial harm to the plaintiff, including forcing the plaintiff to borrow funds at 7.25% high interest to complete a time-sensitive escrow.
