## 📘 Vocabulary Workshop Level C – Unit 2 정답 & 해설

### Part A: Fill-in-the-Blank

1. **minimize** → to make as small as possible; she tried to minimize the crying.
2. **oblique** → indirect or slanting; an oblique attitude feels evasive.
3. **venerate** → to respect deeply; the knight vowed to venerate his lord.
4. **incognito** → in disguise; hard to recognize.
5. **avowed** → declared openly; the lawyer’s avowed argument was clear.

------

### Part B: Synonyms

1. **a) joke** → synonym of *banter* (playful talk).
2. **a) crowded** → synonym of *congested* (overfilled).
3. **b) long-lasting** → synonym of *durable* (not easily worn out).

------

### Part C: Antonyms

1. **b) benefit** → antonym of *detriment* (harm vs. good result).
2. **c) unknown** → antonym of *legendary* (famous vs. not known).

------

### Part D: Multiple Choice in Context

1. **maim** → to injure or cripple; the coach did not want to maim his players emotionally.
2. **gingerly** → with extreme care; students gingerly tried to persuade teacher.
3. **invalidate** → to cancel or disprove; referee invalidated the goal.

------

### Part E: Matching

1. **Avowed → a) declared openly**
2. **Maim → c) to cripple or injure**
3. **Enterprising → d) ambitious, energetic**
4. **Frugal → b) thrifty, economical**

------

### Part F: Sentence Rewrite

1. He **avowed** his support for the cause.
2. The company made **durable** products.
3. She was very **frugal** with her money.
