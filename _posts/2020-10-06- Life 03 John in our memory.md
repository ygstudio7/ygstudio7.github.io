To Trish and John,



We are deeply sorry for your loss and our hearts were broken to hear it yesterday.



We are so sad we couldn't spend more time with John.

We couldn't forget the moment when there was a letter and a flower at the door.

It was the first time someone greeted us in the U.S. since we moved from Korea.

Unfortunately at the time we only had the mind to survive here.

The kind letter and beautiful flower melted our cold hearts and made us feel comfortable like our Korean home.

It made us look forward to the moment we share.

It was just... It was only a year ago, but suddenly everything changed quickly.




The continuing unbelievable facts keep us sad.

If it's not Covid-19,

If the air pollution from wildfires is not severe,

he would be fine, and we may have a good time together as usual.



But, the only thing we can do in our poor English is to thank John very much for giving us good memories.

We will always miss and never forget the British gentleman John.

He's in our hearts and we're here for you.

We pray with our whole hearts for John.
