***\*Change\** the \**Default\** Sending Account in \**Gmail\****

1. In your **Gmail** inbox screen, select **Settings** (gear icon).

2. Select See All **Settings**.

   ![image-20210331091537419](e:\_ygkim\_Doc\_future\\_work\github pages\ygstudio7\ygstudio7.github.io\images\image-20210331091537419.png)

3. Select Accounts and Import.

4. In the Send **mail** as section, choose the **email** you want to use as your **default** address and select **Make Default**.

   ![Gmail Settings screen with Make Default highlighted](https://www.lifewire.com/thmb/461ZKZiQyhw-0T7yuur00IGia1Y=/1733x1014/filters:no_upscale():max_bytes(150000):strip_icc():format(webp)/4MakeDefaultannotated-3ceb874a8290431bb95b04b0a1de4ad8.jpg)

5. You've **set** your new **default** sending address.
