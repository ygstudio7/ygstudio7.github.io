### 2. Select the Loudness Equalizer option in Windows

1. Press Cortana’s taskbar button, and enter the keyword **audio** in the app’s search box.
2. Select **Manage audio devices** to open the window directly below.[![img](https://cdn.windowsreport.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/laptop-volume.png)](https://cdn.windowsreport.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/laptop-volume.png)
3. Select **Speakers** and press the **Properties** button to open the General tab shown in the snapshot below.[![img](https://cdn.windowsreport.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/laptop-audio2.png)](https://cdn.windowsreport.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/laptop-audio2.png)
4. Select the **Enhancement** tab on the **Speakers Properties** window.[![img](https://cdn.windowsreport.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/laptop-audio3.png)](https://cdn.windowsreport.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/laptop-audio3.png)
5. Select the **Loudness Equalizer** checkbox on the **Enhancement** tab.
6. Press the **Apply** > **OK** buttons to close the window.

First, check out the **Loudness Equalizer** option in Windows. Selecting that setting will raise the volume a little above its default maximum for PCs with Realtek audio drivers.

If the Windows 10 volume is too low, you can [check out this guide](https://windowsreport.com/pc-sound-volume-too-low/) to find out how to solve the problem.

**Update**: It seems like Microsoft has removed Loudness Equalizer from the audio settings. To find out more info on the subject, [take a look at this article](https://windowsreport.com/kb4497934-bugs/).
