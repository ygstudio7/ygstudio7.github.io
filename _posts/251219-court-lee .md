The defendant informed the plaintiff that he was performing renovation work for the seller of a property located at **10106 Capistrano Ave, South Gate, CA 90280**. The defendant stated that the seller required a **$10,000 deposit** to secure purchase priority. He assured the plaintiff that the money was safe, that there was no risk of loss, and that he would provide his own check as collateral.

He told the plaintiff that the seller would decide whether to proceed with the sale by early July and asked the plaintiff to wait.
